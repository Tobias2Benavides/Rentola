# User-to-User Rental Agreement

**Version 1 — Effective October 8, 2026**

This Agreement applies between the **lister** (the item's owner) and the **renter** for every booking made through Rentify. Rentify is not a party to this Agreement — see our [Terms of Service](/terms) for Rentify's own role. You accept this Agreement separately for each booking, before it is sent to the lister.

## 1. Rental period

The rental runs from the start date to the end date agreed at booking. The renter must return the item by the agreed end date and time, at the agreed location, unless both parties agree to an extension beforehand.

## 2. Handover

At pickup, both parties should inspect the item together, note its condition, and — using Rentify's handover tool — each upload a timestamped photo of the item's condition and confirm "condition confirmed" or flag an issue with a description. This protects both sides if a dispute arises later.

## 3. Deposit

A deposit, if any, is agreed directly between the lister and the renter before or at handover — Rentify does not set, collect, or hold deposits. Any deposit terms (amount, how it's returned, conditions for withholding it) should be agreed in writing (e.g. in the booking message) before handover.

## 4. Return and condition check

At return, both parties should again inspect the item together and use Rentify's handover tool to upload a timestamped photo and confirm "condition confirmed" or flag an issue. The renter is responsible for returning the item in the condition it was received, normal wear and tear excepted.

## 5. Late return

If the item is not returned by the agreed end date, the lister should contact the renter immediately through Rentify's messaging. Rentify's platform may apply a late-return charge at the listing's daily rate for each additional day, where the renter has a saved payment method; this does not replace the parties' own right to agree compensation for a late return directly.

## 6. Damage, loss, or theft

If the item is damaged, lost, or stolen during the rental:

- the renter must report it to the lister immediately, through Rentify's messaging or the handover tool;
- if the item was lost or stolen, the renter must also report it to the police and share the police report reference with the lister;
- the parties should assess the cost of repair, or — if the item cannot reasonably be repaired — its **current second-hand value immediately before the damage** (not its original purchase price or replacement-as-new value), as the basis for compensation;
- where the parties cannot agree, either may use Rentify's [report tool](/contact) to flag the booking, and Rentify may, at its discretion, help facilitate a resolution on a non-binding basis (see Terms, Section 12).

## 7. Communication

Parties should keep rental-related communication on Rentify's messaging wherever possible, so there's a record if a dispute arises. Agreeing to move payment or communication off-platform does not change either party's obligations under this Agreement.

## 8. No insurance, no warranty from Rentify

Rentify provides no insurance and makes no warranty about the item, the lister, or the renter. This Agreement is between the two users; see the Terms of Service for Rentify's limitation of liability for its own role as a platform.
