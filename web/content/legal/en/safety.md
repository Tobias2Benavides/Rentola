# Safety Tips

**Version 1 — Effective October 8, 2026**

A few practical habits make renting between strangers much safer. None of this is legal advice — just good sense.

## Before you meet

- Agree on a pickup location, time, and (if any) deposit amount **in writing**, through Rentify's messaging, before you meet.
- Check the other person's profile, reviews, and rating.

## At handover

- **Meet in a public place** where possible, especially for a first rental with someone new.
- **Photograph the item** at pickup and at return using Rentify's handover tool, and tick "condition confirmed" or flag an issue — this protects both of you if a dispute comes up later.
- You can **check someone's ID by looking at it** to confirm who you're meeting — but never photograph, copy, or ask to keep a copy of someone's ID.
- Agree any deposit **before** handing over the item, not after.

## Payment

- **Only pay through the methods agreed on the booking** — Stripe Checkout, or the exact payment instructions (IBAN / Tikkie) the lister put on their profile. Never send money to a different account, link, or "agent" someone asks you to use instead.
- Be wary of anyone who pressures you to pay off-platform, pay in advance for something you haven't seen, or pay via an unusual method (gift cards, crypto, wire transfer to a third party).

## During the rental

- Use the item with reasonable care and keep an eye on the return date.
- Message the other party through Rentify if anything comes up — it keeps a record.

## If something feels wrong

- **Report a suspicious user, listing, message, or booking** using the Report button — it's available throughout the app and can be used even if you're not sure something's actually wrong.
- If an item is lost or stolen, report it to the police as well as to the other party.
- If you ever feel unsafe meeting someone, don't go — cancel and report it instead.
