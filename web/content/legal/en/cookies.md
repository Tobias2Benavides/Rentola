# Cookie Policy

**Version 1 — Effective October 8, 2026**

Rentify uses only **strictly necessary (functional) cookies**. We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies or similar technologies, and none load anywhere on the site.

## Cookies we use

| Name | Set by | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sb-*` (Supabase session cookies) | Supabase Auth, via this site | Keeps you signed in and identifies your session | Until sign-out or expiry (see Terms) |

Because these cookies are strictly necessary for the service you've asked for (staying signed in), Dutch and EU cookie rules do not require a consent banner for them. If we ever add analytics, advertising, or any other non-essential cookie, we will add a consent banner before it loads and update this page first.

## Local storage

Some pages may use your browser's local storage for short-lived, non-identifying UI state (such as a draft form field). This never leaves your browser and is not shared with Rentify or any third party.
