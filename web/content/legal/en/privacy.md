# Privacy Policy

**Version 1 — Effective October 8, 2026**

## Controller

Rentify is operated by Tobias Benavides, a private individual, who is the data controller for the personal data described below. Contact: [CONTACT_EMAIL]. Registered address: [ADDRESS – TBD].

## 1. What we collect

- **Account data**: your email address and password (we never see your password itself — it's handled by Supabase Auth).
- **Profile data**: display name, bio, avatar photo, and — if you add it — payment instructions such as an IBAN or Tikkie link.
- **Listing data**: title, description, category, price, city, and photos.
- **Booking data**: rental requests, dates, amounts, payment status, and messages exchanged with the other party.
- **Handover photos**: timestamped photos and condition notes you or the other party upload at pickup and return.
- **Reports and blocks**: reports you file or that are filed about you, and blocks you create.
- **Technical/log data**: standard web server logs (IP address, browser user agent, timestamps) generated by our hosting provider.

We do not collect copies of identity documents or your BSN (Dutch social security number), and never ask for either.

## 3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with the processors that make Rentify work:

- **Supabase** (EU-hosted) — database, authentication, and file storage.
- **Stripe** — payment processing for rentals paid via Stripe Connect.
- **Resend** (EU-hosted) — sends transactional email notifications about your account and rentals.
- **Vercel** — hosts the website.

Other Rentify users see what you'd expect: your display name, avatar, bio, listings, and reviews. They never see your email address, password, or payment instructions unless you choose to share them directly (e.g. a Tikkie link, shown once a rental is approved).

## 4. International transfers

Our processors are chosen to be EU-based where practical (Supabase, Resend). Where a processor or sub-processor transfers data outside the EEA, it does so under an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) under its own data-processing terms.

## 5. Retention

- Account and profile data is kept while your account is active.
- Handover photos are deleted **[6 months — placeholder, confirm with operator]** after the rental they relate to ends.
- Closed reports (dismissed or actioned) are anonymized **[12 months — placeholder, confirm with operator]** after closure; open reports and those tied to an active dispute are kept until resolved.
- If you delete your account, we anonymize your profile and disable sign-in; some records (such as past bookings and reviews involving other users, or data needed for an open dispute or a legal obligation like tax records) are retained in anonymized form rather than deleted outright, so we don't erase other users' own history.
