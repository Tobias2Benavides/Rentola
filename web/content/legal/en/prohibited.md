# Prohibited Items & Conduct

**Version 1 — Effective October 8, 2026**

This page is part of our [Terms of Service](/terms). Listing a prohibited item, or engaging in prohibited conduct, may result in listing removal or account suspension under Terms Section 9.

## Prohibited items

You may not list:

- **Anything requiring motor-vehicle insurance or a driving licence to legally operate**, including scooters, mopeds (brommers), speed pedelecs, cars, motorcycles, and other motorised vehicles.
- **Weapons**, including firearms, airsoft or replica weapons, knives restricted under Dutch law, and ammunition.
- **Drugs, alcohol, and tobacco products.**
- **Medicines and medical devices requiring a prescription.**
- **Stolen or recalled goods.**
- **Counterfeit items**, or items infringing someone else's intellectual property.
- **Hazardous materials**, including flammable, toxic, explosive, or radioactive substances.
- **Live animals.**
- **Any item you do not own or are not entitled to rent out.**
- **Access to illegal services** of any kind.

## Prohibited conduct

You may not use Rentify to:

- harass, threaten, or discriminate against another user;
- commit fraud, or attempt to scam another user (including asking to move payment off-platform to avoid buyer/seller protections, where relevant);
- create fake listings, fake accounts, or fake reviews;
- discriminate against another user on the basis of a protected characteristic.
