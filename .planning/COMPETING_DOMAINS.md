# Competing "Rentify" domains — watchlist

Tracking other companies/sites already operating under the "Rentify" name, found while
setting up rentify.nl. Not our sites — just kept here so we notice if any of them
shift into direct competition, send a takedown/trademark notice, or otherwise become
relevant. Re-check periodically and update the "Last checked" line.

## rentify.life — HIGHEST WATCH: same business model, same country

- **What it is:** Peer-to-peer rental marketplace — people list personal belongings
  for others to rent. AI-assisted listing (scan an item, auto-recognize it, suggest
  pricing), price optimization based on market demand, insurance/deposit coverage.
- **Who runs it:** Rentify BV, Amsterdam, Netherlands. Contact: hello@rentify.life.
  Active on Facebook and Instagram (@rentify.p2p). Copyright notice: © 2025 Rentify.
- **Why it matters:** This is the closest match to our own product — same P2P rental
  concept, same country. Real risk of brand confusion or a trademark conflict, not
  just a coincidental name overlap.
- **Last checked:** 2026-08-30

## rentify.shop — lower watch: different business model

- **What it is:** B2B SaaS for OTHER people's rental/recommerce businesses — storefront
  themes, booking/inventory system, payment processing, reverse logistics (returns,
  cleaning, repairs), Shopify app integration. Not a marketplace itself; sells the
  infrastructure to run one. Pricing: $5–$599/month tiers plus transaction fees.
- **Who runs it:** Solutions Carousel Pte. Ltd., Singapore. Contact: ask.rentify@outlook.com
  (general), hannah.rentify@outlook.com (partnerships).
- **Why it matters:** Same name, different layer of the market (tooling vendor vs.
  marketplace) and different geography. Lower collision risk than rentify.life, but
  still worth knowing it exists.
- **Last checked:** 2026-08-30

## rentify.xyz — lower watch: small website-builder tool

- **What it is:** "Rentify | Rental Website Builder" — a Wix-built site pitching itself
  as a fast, low-cost way to build a rental business website. Looks like a small/simple
  operation (built on Wix, not custom infrastructure).
- **Who runs it:** Not identified — no company name surfaced on the fetched page.
- **Why it matters:** Same B2B "build a rental site" angle as rentify.shop, smaller
  scale. Lowest concern of the three, but same name.
- **Last checked:** 2026-08-30
